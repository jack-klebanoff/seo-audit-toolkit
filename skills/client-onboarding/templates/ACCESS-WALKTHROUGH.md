# Getting Us Access — {{BUSINESS_NAME}}

_A step-by-step guide, not a checklist for us to fill out — this one's
for you. Most of these take 2-5 minutes each. None of them require
handing over a password or giving up ownership of anything — every step
below adds us as a collaborator, on an account that stays yours._

**Why we're asking now, before real work starts:** one of these steps
(Google Business Profile verification, if you don't already have one)
can take days to weeks once it's kicked off, and there's no way to speed
it up — only a way to avoid losing that time by starting late. Everything
else here is fast. If you're not sure about something, skip it and flag
it to us rather than guessing — wrong info costs more time to fix than
it saves.

---

## 1. Google Business Profile

If you already show up on Google Maps when someone searches your
business name, you likely have one of these already — good, this step
is quick.

1. Go to **business.google.com** and sign in with the account that
   manages your listing (if you're not sure which one, search your
   business name on Google Maps, click your listing, and look for
   "Own this business?")
2. Click your business, then **Users** (sometimes under a menu/settings
   icon)
3. Click **Add user**, enter: `{{YOUR_TEAM_EMAIL}}`
4. Set the role to **Manager**
5. Done — no verification needed, since the listing's already yours.

**If you don't have one yet:** tell us. Claiming a brand-new listing is
a longer process (Google verifies you're the real business — by mail,
phone, or video), and it's the one step in this whole list worth
starting today rather than waiting.

## 2. Google Analytics (GA4)

Tells us how people are actually finding and using your site — helps us
know what's working, not just guess.

1. Go to **analytics.google.com** and sign in
2. Click **Admin** (gear icon, bottom left)
3. Under the property for your website, click **Property Access
   Management**
4. Click the **+** button → **Add users**
5. Enter: `{{YOUR_TEAM_EMAIL}}`, role: **Editor**
6. Click **Add**

**If you don't have GA4 set up yet:** no problem — we'll help you get
it wired up once the site's ready, not something to hold anything up
now.

## 3. Google Search Console

Shows us how Google actually sees your site — what's indexed, what's
not, and any errors Google's flagging that you'd otherwise never know
about.

1. Go to **search.google.com/search-console**
2. Select your site (or if it's not there yet, we'll walk you through
   verifying it once we're set up on your domain)
3. Click **Settings** → **Users and permissions**
4. Click **Add user**, enter: `{{YOUR_TEAM_EMAIL}}`
5. Permission level: **Full**

## 4. Your Domain (DNS)

This is the one step people worry about most, so to be clear up front:
**we're not asking you to transfer your domain to us.** You keep it
registered in your name, forever — we just need the ability to point it
at your website.

1. Check who manages your domain's DNS — this is usually wherever you
   bought it (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google Domains, etc.) or, if it's
   already set up that way, Cloudflare
2. **If it's already on Cloudflare:** log into **dash.cloudflare.com**
   → select your domain → **Manage Account** → **Members** → **Invite
   Member** → enter `{{YOUR_TEAM_EMAIL}}` → role: **Administrator** for
   that domain only
3. **If it's not on Cloudflare yet:** just send us the name of your
   registrar and we'll walk you through the fastest path — sometimes
   that's adding us as a collaborator there directly, sometimes it's
   moving DNS management to Cloudflare (free, and makes future changes
   much easier for both of us)
4. **Nothing about your site changes until you say go.** We'll never
   point your live domain at anything new without you explicitly
   signing off first — see the Scope of Work / Client Agreement for
   that commitment in writing.

## 5. A Few Things About Your Business

Not a login, just information — helps us make sure everything we
publish is accurate:

- Your business name exactly as you want it shown publicly
- Real phone number and address (or service area, if you don't have a
  storefront)
- Real hours, including any emergency/24-7 availability
- Any license or certification numbers relevant to your trade
- Real photos, if you have them (we'll never use fake ones without
  telling you)
- Any reviews or testimonials you're comfortable us featuring (never
  fabricated — if you don't have any yet, that's fine, we'll just say
  so rather than make something up)
