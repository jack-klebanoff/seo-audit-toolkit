We're Junefruit, an SEO agency for local service businesses — the kind
of business that lives or dies by whether someone searching "[your
trade] near me" actually finds you, clicks, and calls.

Two ways we work with a business, depending on what you've already got:

**If your current site is solid** — we don't rebuild what isn't broken.
We audit it, fix the technical gaps that are actually costing you
visibility (missing structured data, broken links, slow-loading pages,
duplicate content Google can't tell apart), and layer in the SEO work
your site needs but probably doesn't have. Your site, your look, your
words where they're already working — we're not touching what isn't
the problem.

**If your current site isn't pulling its weight** — outdated, hard to
find, not built for how people actually search anymore, or you don't
have one — we build a new one from the ground up, with the technical
SEO foundation built in from day one instead of bolted on after.

Either way, the same audit and methodology drive the decision — we
don't guess, we run a real technical check first and show you exactly
what it found before recommending which path makes sense for you.

## What you can expect from us

- **We tell you what we find, including the bad news.** If your site's
  fine and doesn't need a rebuild, we'll say so — we're not trying to
  sell you more than you need.
- **Nothing gets published without your sign-off.** Real content, real
  business info, no invented reviews or fabricated credentials — ever.
- **We ask for access, never ownership.** Your domain, your Google
  Business Profile, your analytics — you keep them. We ask to be added
  as a collaborator so we can do the work, not to take them over.
- **You'll know what's happening and when.** No black box — you'll get
  a plain-language update at each real milestone, not just a bill.
