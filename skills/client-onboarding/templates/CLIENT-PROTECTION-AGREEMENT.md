## The commitment, in plain terms

We're being trusted with real access to your website, your Google
Business Profile, your analytics, and your domain's DNS. Here's what we
commit to in exchange for that trust:

1. **We won't touch what's working.** If your current site is staying
   (see "Which track applies" below), we keep your existing design,
   copy, and structure as the baseline. Changes we make are scoped to
   the technical SEO work described in the Scope of Work — not a
   redesign in disguise.
2. **Nothing goes live without your sign-off.** No content changes,
   no domain cutover, no public-facing change of any kind gets
   published without you explicitly approving it first, except routine
   technical fixes explicitly listed as pre-approved in the Scope of
   Work (e.g. adding missing structured data, fixing broken links).
3. **We will not fabricate anything.** No invented reviews, no fake
   testimonials, no made-up credentials, license numbers, or business
   claims. If real information isn't available yet, we'll say so
   plainly rather than filling the gap with something false.
4. **We ask for access, never ownership.** Your domain stays registered
   in your name. Your Google Business Profile, GA4, and Search Console
   accounts stay yours. We're added as a collaborator/manager, which
   you can revoke at any time — see "Ending this agreement" below.
5. **We'll tell you the truth about your site, even when it costs us
   the sale.** If an audit finds your site doesn't need major work, we
   say that. We don't manufacture problems to justify a bigger
   invoice.

## What happens if something goes wrong

- If a change we make causes a real, verifiable problem with your
  site's function or your business (not just "we disagree on how it
  looks"), we fix it at our cost, on our own timeline priority, not
  yours to chase down.
- We carry no ability to lock you out of anything — you can revoke our
  access to any of the accounts above at any time, for any reason, and
  we will not use that as grounds to abandon in-progress work without
  reasonable notice on both sides.
